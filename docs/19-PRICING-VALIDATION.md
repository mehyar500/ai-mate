# Amorien Pricing & Unit Economics Analysis

## 1. Competitor Pricing Landscape
Research on major AI companion apps reveals the market anchor is around $14 - $20/month base:
* **Replika**: $19.99/mo (or $70/year, ~$5.83/mo) - Free tier available.
* **Nomi.ai**: $15.99/mo (or $100/year, ~$8.33/mo) - Free tier available.
* **Kindroid**: $13.99 - $14.99/mo (or annual for ~$9.99/mo)
* **Kin**: Broadly comparable around ~$15/mo.

## 2. Proposed "Aura" Credit System
To integrate high-cost media (voice at $0.033/min, video at $0.145/min) while protecting margins, we use an economy where **1 Aura = $0.01 retail value**:
* **Voice**: `8 Aura / min` ($0.08 retail value vs. $0.033 cost).
* **Video**: `30 Aura / min` ($0.30 retail value vs. $0.145 cost).

## 3. Tier Structure and Unit Economics
*(Assuming 20% blended App Store/Stripe fee, $2.00/month LLM text infrastructure cost, 70% monthly credit utilization by users, and a 60/40 voice/video split of spent credit.)*

### Base Tier: $14.99/mo
* **Includes**: Unlimited Text + 1,000 Aura (~125 voice mins OR ~33 video mins).
* **Net Revenue**: $11.99 (after fees)
* **Est. Media + Text COGS**: $5.09
* **Gross Profit**: **$6.91 per user / 46.1% Margin**

### Pro Tier: $24.99/mo
* **Includes**: Unlimited Text + 2,500 Aura (~312 voice mins OR ~83 video mins).
* **Net Revenue**: $19.99 (after fees)
* **Est. Media + Text COGS**: $9.71
* **Gross Profit**: **$10.28 per user / 41.1% Margin**

## 4. Target Calculation: To Reach $10,000/mo Net Profit
Assuming a mix of **70% Base tier** and **30% Pro tier** users:
* **Blended Profit per User**: **$7.92 / month**
* **MAU Target**: **Exactly 1,264 Active Paying Users** are needed to generate $10,000 in monthly net profit.
