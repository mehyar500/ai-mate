# Bootstrap Viral Go-To-Market (GTM)

## The $15,000/Month Profit Math
* **Subscription Price:** $49.99/month
* **Blended Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** ~$18/month per user (Assuming the "gym membership effect" where a few power users burn heavy compute, but the silent majority uses minor text/limited voice).
* **Blended Net Profit Margin:** ~$32/month per user.
* **Target:** ~470 active paying users to achieve $15,000/month pure profit.

## The $500/Month Zero-Ad-Spend Strategy
Do not spend a $500 budget on Google/Meta ads. Paid search CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) is ~$20-30; this budget would only buy you ~20 users. You will bleed out before scaling.

Instead, allocate the $500 entirely to **generative API compute for viral organic content.** 

### The Playbook: Product as Marketing
Since your product generates hyper-realistic video and audio natively, use the platform as its own marketing engine.
1. **The Format:** POV (Point of View) videos. Example: *"POV: You finally have someone who remembers every little detail about your day."*
2. **The Output:** Generate 3-5 short-form videos daily via your cheap $0.06/min internal API flow.
3. **The Distribution:** Push simultaneously to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. 
4. **The Advantage:** Zero human actors, zero camera equipment, infinite iteration.

### Expected Timeline to $15k
* **Months 1-3:** Algorithmic calibration. Getting 20-50 subscribers while testing hooks.
* **Months 4-6:** Finding the viral format. Spikes push the user base to 200-250.
* **Months 7-9:** Algorithmic snowball effect and peer word-of-mouth. Crossing the 470-user threshold to reach $15k/mo profit.
